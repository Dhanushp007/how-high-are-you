## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-octane Late-Night Neon Arcade aesthetic, blending glassmorphic depth with aggressive, luminous accents against an obsidian void. The target audience consists of night-owl gamers and digital natives seeking immediate gratification, high-energy interactions, and an immersive, futuristic environment. 

The emotional response should be one of electrifying excitement, laser focus, and nocturnal playfulness. Visual hierarchy relies on stark contrast: deep dark mode surfaces paired with vibrant, saturated neon highlights. The underlying structural style merges **Glassmorphism** with **High-Contrast / Bold** principles, utilizing frosted, semi-transparent layers punctuated by razor-sharp glowing borders and vivid chromatic pulses.

## Colors

The color palette is anchored by a deep obsidian background (`#09090B`) that establishes infinite dark space. Glassmorphic containers sit atop this void using elevated dark surfaces (`#18181B`) with semi-transparent alpha overlays. 

Three primary neon tones carve through the dark:
- **Acid Lime (`#CCFF00`)**: Drives primary actions, successful state validations, and win conditions.
- **Hot Magenta (`#FF007F`)**: Signals critical warnings, high-stakes dares, and destructive actions.
- **Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`)**: Reserved exclusively for dynamic metrics, countdown timers, and data visualization.

Neutrals scale from pure black to crisp white, with structural borders utilizing 1px translucent white or tinted neon strokes to catch the light.

## Typography

Typography fuses bold, chunky geometric headers with razor-sharp utilitarian data tracking. `Space Grotesk` drives high-impact display headers, offering a wide, futuristic posture that commands attention. `Syne` provides an expressive, contemporary voice for body copy and general UI text.
